What is Maki?

Maki is an all-in-one Telegram group manager bot. Add it to your group, make it admin, and it quietly takes care of moderation, security and member engagement — so you can focus on your community instead of babysitting the chat.

Key Features

👋 Welcome & CaptchaGreets new members and verifies they're human before they can chat.
🛡️ Anti-Spam & Anti-RaidStops spam floods and coordinated raid attacks automatically.
🔒 LocksLock links, stickers, media, forwards and more — per group.
🚫 BlocklistBanned words are auto-deleted; repeat offenders get punished.
⚠️ WarningsWarn misbehaving members; auto-action after the limit is hit.
📝 Notes & FiltersSave reusable replies and auto-answer common questions.
🌙 Night ModeAutomatically locks the group at night and opens it in the morning.
🌐 FederationsLink multiple groups and ban troublemakers everywhere at once.
💬 Quote StickersTurn any message into a beautiful quote sticker with /q.
📈 Reports & StatsMembers can report problems; admins see group activity stats.

The /start Panel

When someone opens Maki in DM and sends /start, they see a poster photo with this caption:

🌸 मᵀKì

Your group's silent guardian — keeping every chat clean, safe and fun, day and night.

Add me to your group, sit back and relax — I'll handle the rest. 💫

Below the caption sit four buttons:

Add me to your group — opens Telegram's group picker; Maki joins the chosen group and starts working instantly.
Help — opens the full command menu with 17 sections covering every feature.
Support — opens a direct chat with the owner (@RohanDevta) for help or questions.
Owner — also opens @RohanDevta; support and owner are the same person.

For Admins

Group admins get simple one-word commands — ban, mute, warn, pin, purge, lock, and more. Everything is documented inside the bot's Help menu, so nothing needs to be memorised.

Owner & Sudo System

The bot has a two-level trust system. The owner controls everything, including the most powerful commands (mass actions, global bans, broadcast). Trusted people can be added as sudo users with /addsudo — they can run operational commands like /broadcast, /groups and /restrict, while owner-only commands stay locked. /listsudo shows the current team.
