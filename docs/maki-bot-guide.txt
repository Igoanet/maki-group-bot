------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS MAKI?
------------------------------------------------------------
Maki is an all-in-one Telegram group manager bot. Add it to
your group, make it admin, and it quietly takes care of
moderation, security and member engagement — so you can
focus on your community instead of babysitting the chat.


------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------
* Welcome & Captcha
  Greets new members and verifies they're human before
  they can chat.

* Anti-Spam & Anti-Raid
  Stops spam floods and coordinated raid attacks
  automatically.

* Locks
  Lock links, stickers, media, forwards and more —
  per group.

* Blocklist
  Banned words are auto-deleted; repeat offenders get
  punished.

* Warnings
  Warn misbehaving members; auto-action after the limit
  is hit.

* Notes & Filters
  Save reusable replies and auto-answer common questions.

* Night Mode
  Automatically locks the group at night and opens it in
  the morning.

* Federations
  Link multiple groups and ban troublemakers everywhere
  at once.

* Quote Stickers
  Turn any message into a beautiful quote sticker with /q.

* Reports & Stats
  Members can report problems; admins see group activity
  stats.


------------------------------------------------------------
THE /start PANEL
------------------------------------------------------------
When someone opens Maki in DM and sends /start, they see a
poster photo with this caption:

    MAKI

    Your group's silent guardian — keeping every chat
    clean, safe and fun, day and night.

    Add me to your group, sit back and relax — I'll
    handle the rest.

Below the caption sit four buttons:

1. Add me to your group (green)
   Opens Telegram's group picker; Maki joins the chosen
   group and starts working instantly.

2. Help
   Opens the full command menu with 17 sections covering
   every feature.

3. Support
   Opens a direct chat with the owner (@RohanDevta) for
   help or questions.

4. Owner
   Also opens @RohanDevta; support and owner are the same
   person.


------------------------------------------------------------
FOR ADMINS
------------------------------------------------------------
Group admins get simple one-word commands — ban, mute,
warn, pin, purge, lock, and more. Everything is documented
inside the bot's Help menu, so nothing needs to be
memorised.


------------------------------------------------------------
OWNER & SUDO SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------------
The bot has a two-level trust system.

The OWNER controls everything, including the most powerful
commands (mass actions, global bans, broadcast).

Trusted people can be added as SUDO USERS with /addsudo —
they can run operational commands like /broadcast, /groups
and /restrict, while owner-only commands stay locked.

/listsudo shows the current team.
